Before you begin

Six things to set up. They build on each other, so please do them in order. Part 1 is the important one; everything after it is measurement.

What you need

  • A computer with a web browser. A phone will not work for this.
  • Your Google account, signed in.
  • About 45 minutes in total. You can stop between parts and come back.
  • A quiet hour. Some steps involve copying long codes, and it is easy to lose one character.
You cannot break anything

Nothing in this guide deletes data or costs money. If a screen does not look like the description, stop and send us a screenshot rather than guessing. That is always the right move.

A note on the words. A few names come up repeatedly. Netlify is the company that stores your website and shows it to visitors. GoDaddy is where your web address workingwithgod.live is registered. Google Apps Script is a small program that lives inside your own spreadsheet. You do not need to understand any of them beyond that.

What you will have when you finish

  • 02The new website is live on the internet
    On a temporary address first, so we can check it safely
  • 03workingwithgod.live points at the new site
    Your email keeps working throughout
  • 04Google Analytics tells you who visits
    How many people, from where, which pages
  • 05Google Search Console shows how people find you

Part 01

Registrations into your inbox and a spreadsheet

Right now, when someone registers for the Roundtable, nothing happens on the new site. This part makes two things happen at once: an email arrives for you, and a row appears in a spreadsheet that belongs to you.

About 15 minutes

Step by step

  1. 01

    Go to sheets.google.com and click the big + to make a blank spreadsheet.

    Name it WWG Registrations by clicking "Untitled spreadsheet" at the top left.

  2. 02

    Look at the web address at the top of your browser. It is long. You need the middle part of it.

    The address looks like thisdocs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1AbC…longcode…XyZ/edit

    Copy the bold part, between /d/ and /edit. That is your Sheet ID. Paste it somewhere safe for a moment.

  3. 03

    In the spreadsheet menu, click Extensions, then Apps Script. A new tab opens with a code editor.

    Do not be put off by this. You will paste something in and never look at it again.

  4. 04

    Select everything already in that editor and delete it. Then paste in the file we sent you called Code.gs.

  5. 05

    Near the top of what you pasted, find three lines and fill them in. Keep the quote marks.

    Change these three SHEET_ID  = 'paste your Sheet ID here'
    NOTIFY_TO = 'eliyahu@workingwithgod.live'
    SECRET    = 'a long random password you invent'

    For SECRET, type a long jumble of letters and numbers. Twenty characters or more. You will need it again in one minute, so keep it on screen.

  6. 06

    Click the Save icon, then the blue Deploy button at the top right, then New deployment.

    Click the gear icon next to "Select type" and choose Web app.

  7. 07

    Two dropdowns appear. Set them exactly like this:

    These two settings matter Execute as:     Me
    Who has access: Anyone
    "Anyone" — not "Anyone with a Google Account"

    These sit next to each other and the second one sounds safer. It is the wrong choice, and if you pick it, registrations will silently vanish. Read the option twice before clicking Deploy.

  8. 08

    Click Deploy. Google asks you to authorise. Click through, choose your account, and you will hit a warning screen that says Google hasn't verified this app.

    This warning is expected

    It appears for every private script that has not been submitted to Google for review. Yours never will be, because only you use it. Click Advanced at the bottom left, then Go to (your project name), then Allow.

  9. 09

    Google now shows you a Web app URL ending in /exec. Copy it.

    Add your secret to the end of it, with ?key= in between and no spaces anywhere:

    The finished addresshttps://script.google.com/…/exec?key=your-secret-here

    Send that whole address to us. That is the last thing we need from Part 1.

    Treat this address like a password

    Anyone who has it can write rows into your spreadsheet. Do not put it in a screenshot, a shared document, or a social post. Email it to us and leave it there.

How you will know it worked

Once we connect it, register on the site yourself using your own email address. Within a minute you should see an email arrive, and your details appear as a new row on the Roundtable tab of the spreadsheet.

About the spreadsheet. Three tabs are created automatically, one for each kind of form: Roundtable, Contact and Daily quote. There will only ever be those three, however many years pass. Each tab holds only the columns that form actually asks for, so the Roundtable tab is not half full of empty message boxes.

The first column is Month, written as 2026-09. Click the little filter arrow at the top of it to show one month at a time. The last column is Notes and nothing ever writes to it, so it is yours: jot down who turned up, who to follow up with, anything you like.

Part 02

Putting the website live

The new site needs a home on the internet. That is Netlify. We do most of this, but two switches are yours, and the site cannot receive registrations without them.

About 5 minutes
  1. 01

    Sign in at app.netlify.com. You will see your site listed. Click it.

    At the top there is a temporary web address ending in .netlify.app. Click it and have a look around the new site. Nothing here is public yet.

  2. 02

    In the left menu click Site configuration, then Forms.

    Turn on Form detection.

  3. 03

    Now go to Deploys in the top menu, click Trigger deploy, then Deploy site. Wait until it says Published, usually under two minutes.

    Do not skip this rebuild

    Netlify only looks for your form while it is building the site. Turning the switch on does nothing to the version already published. Without this rebuild, the switch is decorative.

  4. 04

    Go to Site configuration, then Notifications, then Emails and webhooks.

    Add a Form submission notification, choose the roundtable form, and enter your email address.

    If this option asks you to upgrade to a paid plan, stop and tell us. There is a free way round it and we have already written it.

Part 03

Pointing workingwithgod.live at the new site

Your web address currently points at the old GoDaddy site. This changes where it points. It is the only step in this guide that carries any real risk, so read the warning first.

About 10 minutes, then a wait
Your email runs on this same address

Because you receive mail at eliyahu@workingwithgod.live, changing the wrong setting here stops your email, quietly, for everyone writing to you. We change only two entries and leave everything else exactly as it is. If GoDaddy ever offers to "change nameservers" or "connect to another host" automatically, say no and call us.

  1. 01

    Sign in at GoDaddy. Find workingwithgod.live in your products, then open DNS or Manage DNS.

    You will see a table of rows with columns like Type, Name and Value. This is the address book that tells the internet where to find your things.

  2. 02

    Find the row where Type is A and Name is @. Edit it and replace the value with:

    New value for the A record75.2.60.5

    If there is more than one A row with name @, delete the extras and keep one.

    If GoDaddy will not let you edit the A record

    The old website builder may still be attached to the domain and holding it. In your GoDaddy products, open the old Websites + Marketing site and disconnect the domain from it. The DNS rows then become editable.

  3. 03

    Find the row where Type is CNAME and Name is www. Change its value to your Netlify address, the one ending .netlify.app.

  4. 04

    Save. Change nothing else. In particular, leave every row of type MX and TXT completely alone. Those are your email.

  5. 05

    Wait. It can take a few minutes or a few hours for the change to spread across the internet.

    During that window the site may show a security warning about its certificate. That is normal and fixes itself once the change has spread.

Cancel the old subscription last, not first

Once the new site loads at workingwithgod.live and you have confirmed your email still arrives, then cancel Websites + Marketing. Cancelling first can take the old site down before the new one is reachable, leaving nothing live in between.

Part 04

Google Analytics: who visits

This tells you how many people come to the site, which pages they read, and roughly where in the world they are. It is free and it is the standard tool everybody uses.

About 8 minutes
  1. 01

    Go to analytics.google.com and sign in. Click Start measuring.

  2. 02

    Account name: Working With God. Accept the defaults on the sharing settings and continue.

  3. 03

    Property name: workingwithgod.live. Set your time zone and currency, then continue.

  4. 04

    Answer the business questions however you like. Nothing here changes how it works. Choose Generate leads if you want a sensible default.

  5. 05

    When it asks about a platform, choose Web. Enter workingwithgod.live as the website URL and give the stream a name.

  6. 06

    You are now shown a Measurement ID. It starts with G- and looks like this:

    Copy this and send it to usG-ABC1234XYZ

    That is all we need. Ignore any instructions Google gives you about installing code.

Part 05

Google Search Console: how people find you

Analytics tells you who arrived. Search Console tells you what they typed into Google first, and whether Google can read your pages properly. Different tool, different question.

About 5 minutes
Do Part 4 first and this becomes much easier

If Google Analytics is already set up on the same Google account, Search Console can usually verify you own the site by itself, with no codes to copy at all.

  1. 01

    Go to search.google.com/search-console and sign in with the same Google account you used for Analytics.

  2. 02

    You are offered two boxes. Use the one on the right, URL prefix, and enter:

    https://workingwithgod.live
  3. 03

    On the verification screen, expand Other verification methods and look for Google Analytics. Choose it and click Verify. It should succeed immediately.

    If that option is missing or fails, stop here and tell us. There is another way that needs a change at GoDaddy, and we would rather make that change than have you do it.

  4. 04

    Once verified, click Sitemaps in the left menu and submit:

    sitemap.xml

    This hands Google a list of every page on the site so it does not have to guess.

Do not expect data tomorrow. Search Console takes a few days to gather anything worth reading, and a few weeks to become genuinely useful. This is normal and is not a sign that something is broken.

Part 06

Microsoft Clarity: how people actually use the site

This is the interesting one. Clarity records anonymous sessions, so you can watch where people scrolled, what they clicked, and the exact point at which they gave up and left. Free, with no limit on traffic.

About 5 minutes
  1. 01

    Go to clarity.microsoft.com and sign in. You can use your Google account.

  2. 02

    Click New project. Name it Working With God, enter https://workingwithgod.live as the website, and pick a category.

  3. 03

    Clarity offers you several ways to install it. Ignore all of them and click Settings, then Overview.

  4. 04

    Find the Clarity project ID. It is a short jumble of letters and numbers:

    Copy this and send it to usabcde12345

On privacy. Clarity masks what people type, so passwords and form contents are not recorded. What you see is movement and clicks, not personal information.

Part 07

The final check

One test proves the whole chain at once. Do this after we confirm we have wired in the codes you sent.

About 3 minutes, then 24 hours of patience
  1. 01

    Go to workingwithgod.live and register for the Roundtable using your own name and email address, exactly as a real visitor would.

  2. 02

    Within a minute or two, check all three of these:

    Where to lookWhat should be there
    Your email inboxA message with the four details you typed
    The spreadsheetA new row on the Roundtable tab
    Netlify, under FormsThe same registration, stored
  3. 03

    Open that email and press Reply. Check that it addresses the person who registered, rather than a no-reply address. This is how you will answer real registrants.

  4. 04

    Now wait a day. Then open Google Analytics and Clarity. Both should show at least one visit, yours. If they are still empty after 48 hours, tell us.

That is everything

Once that test passes, the site is live, registrations reach you two ways, and you can see who is visiting and what they do. Nothing above needs maintaining and nothing renews.

Reference

If something looks wrong

Almost everything that goes wrong here has one of five causes. Check these before assuming something is broken.

What you seeWhat it usually means
Registered, but no email and nothing in the sheet Form detection was switched on without rebuilding the site afterwards. Go back to Part 2, step 3.
Netlify shows the registration, but the sheet stays empty Usually the secret is missing from the address, or access was set to "Anyone with a Google Account" instead of "Anyone".
The website will not load at workingwithgod.live The DNS change has not spread yet. Give it a few hours. If it is still failing the next morning, tell us.
A security or certificate warning on the site Normal in the first minutes after the DNS change. It clears itself.
Analytics or Clarity showing nothing Either we have not added your codes yet, or fewer than 24 hours have passed. Both are common.
Your email stopped arriving Stop and contact us immediately. Do not change anything else at GoDaddy in the meantime.
